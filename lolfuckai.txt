                                     ABOUT
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

  I made this demo to reflect what pouët looked like these days and to put a
  spotlight on the bullying going on in the community.

  AI haters trashing prod pages and the forum, site moderators locking the
  threads instead of allowing any kind of dialogue, and senior sceners who
  have given decades to the demoscene getting disrespected and pushed out
  just for experimenting with new tools.

  So I fed those prod comments and locked forum threads (#13101, #13104,
  #13069) straight back into the machine.

  Some people make demos using someone else's engine and node editor.
  I make demos using my own engine and a chatbot.
